Questions,
answered.

Do you coach natural bodybuilders?
Yes. I coach plenty of natural athletes, Mark included, and I build every plan around the exact division and category you are stepping on stage in.
What does it cost?
Vincere Standard is $450 per month, $2,500 for six months, or $4,200 for twelve months. Vincere Elite is priced on your call, based on what your prep needs. Posing coaching is included in Elite and a separate add-on in Standard.
Is it online or in person?
Coaching is fully online, so I can work with you wherever you are. If you're in the Montreal or Toronto area, in-person sessions are an add-on in Standard and included in Elite.
Do I need to have competed before?
No. First timer or seasoned, everyone is welcome on one condition: you are willing to actually live the lifestyle of a bodybuilder. The standard does not bend.
What is included?
Training, nutrition, supplement guidance, cardio, weekly check-ins, my app, and mindset work, plus direct messaging access to me. Elite adds posing, in-person sessions, priority access, and full peak-week command.
How long do I commit for?
Run month to month, or lock in six or twelve months for a lower monthly cost. Real change takes months, not weeks, so I build for the long game and reward the athletes who commit to it.
