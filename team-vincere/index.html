Before you apply

Questions,
answered.

Do you coach natural bodybuilders?
Yes. I coach plenty of natural athletes, Mark included, and I build every plan around the exact division and category you are stepping on stage in.
What does it cost?
Coaching is $450 per month, $2,500 for six months, or $4,200 for twelve months. The longer you commit, the lower your monthly cost, and the further we can take your physique. Posing coaching is a separate add-on.
Is it online or in person?
Coaching is fully online, so I can work with you wherever you are. If you're in the Montreal or Toronto area, you can add in-person training on top.
Do I need to have competed before?
No. First timer or seasoned, everyone is welcome on one condition: you are willing to actually live the lifestyle of a bodybuilder. The standard does not bend.
What is included?
Training, nutrition, supplement guidance, cardio, weekly check-ins, my app, and mindset work, plus direct messaging access to me. Posing coaching is an optional add-on.
How long do I commit for?
Run month to month at $450, lock in six months at $2,500, or go all in on twelve months at $4,200. Real change takes months, not weeks, so I build for the long game and reward the athletes who commit to it.
