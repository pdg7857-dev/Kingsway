The story

Built to
compete.

I grew up a gymnast, competing across Canada and at both the Canadian and American national level, then coached gymnastics for over six years. I ran track and field in university until the money ran out and I had to walk away. Competing was never a hobby for me. It was who I was.

The gym caught me instantly. I went from 160 to 180 pounds in three months and knew, that fast, that I wanted to turn pro. My first two years I did it wrong. I dirty bulked, I guessed, I wasted time. Year three I locked all the way in, and I have never looked back.

I started personal training in January 2025 and became a bodybuilding coach that August. At the 2025 Atlantic Championships I won my height class and took second in true novice in the CPA. Now I'm building toward the 2027 stage and my pro card. I will never ask you to chase something I'm not chasing myself.
